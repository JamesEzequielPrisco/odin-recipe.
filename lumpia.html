Lumpiang Shanghai

Description

Lumpiang Shanghai is a classic Filipino appetizer made of thin pastry wrappers rolled around a savory filling of seasoned ground pork, finely minced carrots, onions, and garlic. Fried until golden brown and ultra-crispy, these bite-sized spring rolls are a staple at every Filipino celebration and are traditionally served with sweet chili sauce or banana ketchup.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1/2 cup carrots, finely minced
  • 1 medium onion, finely minced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup green onions (scallions), finely chopped
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt and 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 25 pieces spring roll wrappers (lumpia wrappers)
  • 2 cups cooking oil, for deep-frying
  • Sweet chili sauce, for serving

Steps

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the ground pork, minced carrots, onion, garlic, green onions, beaten egg, soy sauce, salt, and black pepper until thoroughly mixed.
  2. Lay one lumpia wrapper flat on a clean surface and spoon about 1 to 2 tablespoons of the pork mixture in a thin line along the bottom edge.
  3. Fold the left and right sides inward over the filling, then roll tightly upward from the bottom, dabbing the top edge with a little water to seal it shut.
  4. Heat the cooking oil in a deep skillet or wok over medium heat until hot.
  5. Fry the lumpia in batches for 3 to 4 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown and crispy all over, then drain on paper towels.
  6. Slice diagonally into bite-sized pieces and serve warm with sweet chili dipping sauce.
