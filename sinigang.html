Pork Sinigang

Description

Pork Sinigang is a beloved Filipino sour soup known for its comforting, savory-tangy tamarind broth. Simmered with tender pork ribs, ripe tomatoes, daikon radish, okra, and leafy water spinach, it is the ultimate rainy-day dish best enjoyed piping hot over steamed white rice.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs pork spareribs or pork belly, cut into serving pieces
  • 1 packet (40g) tamarind soup base mix (sinigang sa sampalok)
  • 8 cups water
  • 3 medium ripe tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 large onion, quartered
  • 1 cup daikon radish (labanos), sliced diagonally
  • 6–8 pieces okra, ends trimmed
  • 1 bunch water spinach (kangkong), leaves and tender stems separated
  • 2 pieces long green chili peppers (siling haba)
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce (patis), or to taste

Steps

  1. Place the pork, quartered tomatoes, and onion in a large pot with 8 cups of water and bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
  2. Skim off any scum or foam that rises to the top, then lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 45 minutes until the pork is tender.
  3. Add the sliced radish and okra, and simmer for 5 minutes until just tender.
  4. Stir in the tamarind soup base mix, fish sauce, and green chili peppers, then simmer for another 2 to 3 minutes.
  5. Turn off the heat, add the kangkong leaves, cover the pot for 2 minutes until wilted, and serve hot with steamed white rice.
