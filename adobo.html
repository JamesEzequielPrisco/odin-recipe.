Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken Adobo is widely considered the unofficial national dish of the Philippines. It features tender chicken pieces marinated and braised in a bold, savory-tangy reduction of soy sauce, vinegar, crushed garlic, bay leaves, and whole black peppercorns until the sauce thickens into a rich, glossy glaze.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs bone-in chicken thighs and drumsticks, cut into serving pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup white or cane vinegar
  • 1 whole head garlic (8–10 cloves), peeled and crushed
  • 3 dried bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon brown sugar (optional)

Steps

  1. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and crushed garlic in a large pot or bowl and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Place the pot over medium-high heat, add the bay leaves and black peppercorns, and bring to a boil.
  3. Pour in the white vinegar and let it boil uncovered for 3 to 5 minutes without stirring so the raw vinegar flavor cooks off.
  4. Lower the heat, cover the pot, and simmer for 25 to 30 minutes until the chicken is tender and the sauce reduces.
  5. Serve hot over steamed white rice.
